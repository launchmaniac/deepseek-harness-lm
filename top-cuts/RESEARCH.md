## Design brief derived from research

1. Lead with trust markers: 9 years, Main Street location, real stylists, real prices.
2. Prices visible up front (their differentiator) — a price list page section, not hidden.
3. Booking flow separates **cuts** (walk-in OK, booking optional) from **color/chemical**
   (book ahead; longer chair time) — mirrors how the shop actually runs.
4. Mobile-first: small-town traffic is overwhelmingly phones.
5. Look handmade-professional, not template-generic.
